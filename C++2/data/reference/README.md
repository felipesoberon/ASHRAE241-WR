## Independent and generated fields

The independently audited publication fields are `source_citation`, `category`, the `source_*` category parameters, and `published_adjusted_l_s_person`. The publication comparator is the adjusted Table 4 value, in L s⁻¹ per person, rather than an unrounded Monte Carlo result.

The `effective_*` fields, run controls, aggregate counters, raw mean and confidence interval, ceiling-to-5 value, differences, comparator status, sampling-design version, and qualification are deterministic C++2 regression data. They were generated by the current `ecai_cpp2` source and do not independently validate its equations, transforms, sampling design, or scientific correctness. The confidence interval is the C++2 approximate randomized-LHS Monte Carlo Student-t interval for the mean replicate-percentile estimator; it is not a publication confidence interval or an interval for a population percentile or individual variation.

## Bounded-run provenance

The retained rows came from these bounded commands, where `<temporary>` was a temporary output directory:

```text
ecai_cpp2 --category "Patient room" --simulations 10000 --replicates 10 --output-directory <temporary>
ecai_cpp2 --category Museum --simulations 10000 --replicates 5 --output-directory <temporary>
```

Both commands used base seed `485` (the current default), percentile `0.96`, confidence level `0.95`, target probability `0.001`, duration `1` hour, sampling-design version `1`, and seed policy `CPP2-SEED-v1`. Patient used `N = 10000`, `R = 10`; Museum used `N = 10000`, `R = 5`.

Generation used C++17 under WSL Ubuntu on x86-64 with `g++ 13.3.0`, glibc `2.39`, and the project flags `-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -fno-fast-math -ffp-contract=off`. The bounded results and statuses are recorded in [`docs/VALIDATION.md`](../../docs/VALIDATION.md). The exact default workload of 10,000 simulations × 500 replicates was not run. No raw per-simulation or full generated summary/replicate outputs were retained; only the reviewed 31-column bounded fixture remains.
